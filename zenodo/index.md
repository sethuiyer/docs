# Zenodo Records

Canonical, citable archives of every ShunyaBar Labs artifact. Each record is assigned a DOI by Zenodo and is the version of record for that artifact. Use these DOIs in academic citations, grant reports, and reproducibility appendices.

**All records are authored by:** Sethurathienam Iyer — [ORCID: 0009-0008-5446-2856](https://orcid.org/0009-0008-5446-2856)

---

## Why Zenodo?

- **Persistent DOIs** that resolve even if our domain changes.
- **Versioning** — every release is a separate citable artifact.
- **Long-term storage** backed by CERN's Data Centre.
- **Open metadata** so search engines, ORCID, and OpenAIRE index our work.

## How to Cite

Each record page contains a ready-to-paste BibTeX entry. For umbrella citation of the lab as a whole, use:

```bibtex
@software{shunyabar-labs-zenodo,
  author       = {Sethurathienam, Iyer},
  title        = {{ShunyaBar Labs Research Artifacts}},
  publisher    = {Zenodo},
  year         = {2025--2026},
  url          = {https://zenodo.org/communities/shunyabar}
}
```

For project-specific records, copy the BibTeX from the record page.
